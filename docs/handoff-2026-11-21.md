## The task: clean up curation, then add presentations

The user's concern: selection grew by accretion and needs proper curation, with different "presentations"
(single, pair, collage, later event grid and others) that cooperate. Full plan: `docs/curation-plan.md`. Summary:

Problems found (verified by reading, and by an independent review):
1. The seasonal half of the playlist has no play history and wraps with `% memories.size`; at defaults a
   40-photo pool repeats 7-8 times per cycle (`PhotoSelection.playlist`).
2. Collages read the whole album via `CollageThemeSelection.bestDay`, bypassing the deck, burst limit and
   priority queue.
3. Three separate "same moment" notions: `Bursts`, `PhotoSimilarity`, same-day grouping.
4. `recentCount` now means deck size but is labelled "recent captures".
5. Orchestration (`rebuild`, `showNext`, `planPreloads`, `ensureSeasonal`) lives untested in `MainActivity`;
   `poolFor` is duplicated in `showNext` and the Preview path in `applyRemote`.
6. `playHistory` is a plain map shared across threads (to verify).

Agreed design (two objects, not four layers): a pure-Kotlin `PlaybackEngine` (the logic of `rebuild`,
`showNext`, `planPreloads`) and a `SlideFactory` with a `Presentation` interface (Single, Pair, Collage).
Split out a `Curator` only when a second scoring signal lands in Phase 4.

Phases: 0 extract + simulation harness (measure repeats, coverage, year histogram, presentation mix,
burst share); 1 fix the memories repeat; 2 one gate for every photo (`claim` is the only place plays are
recorded); 3 presentations as plug-ins, with a side-effect-free `peek()` for preload planning; 4 new
signals and presentations one at a time.
